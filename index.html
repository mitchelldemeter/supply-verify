The ledger is public.
Nobody's reading it.

A listed company can't print a share without a transfer agent recording it, an auditor signing off and a regulator holding the filing. Crypto put every coin on an open ledger, then went back to taking the issuer's word for it. Supply still comes from a docs page, a blog post or an aggregator field the team filled in. This site skips all of that and asks the chain.

Shares

  • Transfer agent keeps the register
  • Audited share count, every year
  • Dilution disclosed in filings
  • Insider trades reported
  • Regulator can halt and sanction

Tokens

  • Team multisig holds the mint key
  • Supply is whatever the docs say
  • Mints land on a Tuesday, unannounced
  • "Burned" can mean parked
  • Nobody checks the counter
The difference: every token mint is already public. It just needs reading.
